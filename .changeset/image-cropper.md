Add `ImageCropper`, `ImageCropperTrigger`, `ImageCropperCropper`, `ImageCropperCrop`, and `ImageCropperCancel` to the primitives, and a styled `image-cropper` to the `@implementjs/ui` registry. Choose a file, pan and zoom it under a fixed crop area with the mouse, touch, or keyboard, and get the cropped PNG back as an object URL. Also exports `getFileFromUrl` and `ImageCropperContext`.
